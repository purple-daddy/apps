Matterleast support

Web, iPhone, Android · TestFlight beta (iPhone), direct download (Android)

What it is

Matterleast is a team chat app that runs on a server your organization owns. People chat in channels, direct messages and threads, share files, and search. It also has work schedules, time cards and a car check-in with photos.

You use it in a web browser or in the iPhone and Android apps.

Getting started

  1. Open the app or web page for your team's chat.
  2. Let the app find your team's server, or enter the server address your administrator gave you.
  3. Sign in with the account your administrator or invite link created.
  4. Allow notifications to get message alerts.
  5. To change servers, use Change server in Settings.

Common questions

The app says it can't reach the server. What now?

Use the Can't reach the server button. It checks the connection and, if you tap send, sends a short report to the developer without using your server. The server may also be down or you may be off its network.

How do I clear Matterleast from my phone?

In Settings choose Erase All Data on This Device. It removes local data only. Your account and messages stay on the server.

How do I delete my account or messages?

The app has no self-service delete. Ask your organization's administrator.

Are messages end-to-end encrypted?

No. The server stores messages in readable form, and administrators of the server can see them. Connections use HTTPS when the server is reached over https.

Where are the logs?

Logs are uploaded to your organization's server. They can be viewed only through a hidden developer area behind a passcode.

Still stuck?

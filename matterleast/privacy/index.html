Matterleast privacy policy

Web, iPhone, Android · TestFlight beta (iPhone), direct download (Android) · Last updated 2026-10-23

Team chat that runs on your own server.

What this app is

Matterleast is a team chat app with channels, direct messages, threads, file sharing and search. It also has tools for a shop: work schedules, time cards and a way to check in a car with photos.

It is self-hosted. Each organization runs its own small server and people use it in a browser or in the iPhone and Android apps. Your data lives on that server, which the developer's own shop runs for its own team. The developer does not run a central service that collects it. The Android app allows device backups that can include the sign-in session, and the apps allow unencrypted connections to servers, so owners should run it only over a private network or HTTPS.

Diagnostic and usage data

Matterleast records diagnostic logs. On your device the app records a heartbeat every 10 seconds, even when idle. The heartbeat has the current screen, app state, your username, network type, memory, storage and battery level, screen size, app version and a session id. The app also records taps, clicks, scrolls, screen changes, pointer movement, key presses, network errors, app launch and close, and errors with details. The server also logs each request, including the IP address of the requester.

The events are queued on your device and uploaded to the server you sign in to. They are not sent to any analytics company. The server keeps them as daily files for 30 days, capped at about 1 GB. Typed text, message text, passwords, passcodes and login tokens are not logged. Key-press events do not include what was typed. Only a hidden developer area, behind a passcode, can view or download the logs. This is Usage Data in App Store terms, and it is used only for fixing problems. Your device is labeled in logs with a random name such as ios-a1b2c3.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No analytics or crash-reporting libraries
  • No location access, and car check-in photos have GPS data removed
  • No contacts or Bluetooth access, and no Face ID use
  • Your data is not sold
  • No paid AI service: text recognition and help use Apple's on-device features

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Your messages, files, schedules and time cards stay on the organization's server. There is no automatic expiry for them today. The server keeps backups: nightly copies for 30 days and copies made before updates. Diagnostic logs are deleted after 30 days. Unsent attachments are cleaned up daily.

To clear this device, open Settings and choose Erase All Data on This Device. It removes the saved server, your sign-in, drafts, the offline copy, the local log queue and this app's push registration. It does not touch the server or your account.

There is no button to delete your account in the app. An administrator can deactivate an account. To ask about your data, contact your organization's administrator.

Children

Matterleast is a workplace tool. Accounts are created by an administrator or an invite link. It is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. To ask about your data, open an issue (don't include private information) or use TestFlight feedback.

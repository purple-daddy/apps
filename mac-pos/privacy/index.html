Mac POS privacy policy

Mac · In development, not released · Last updated 2026-10-23

A point-of-sale register for the Mac, in the style of QuickBooks Point of Sale. All data stays on your Mac.

What this app is

Mac POS is a native Mac register app, modeled on the old QuickBooks Point of Sale 2013 that a repair shop used on a Windows PC. It runs sales with receipts, customers, items and payment types against a database stored on the Mac. It is a separate app and is not a client for any other system.

It is still being built and is not ready for real sales. This page describes what the current code does.

Diagnostic and usage data

Mac POS keeps an activity log on your Mac only. It writes a short line every 10 seconds, even when idle. It also records actions, pointer movement, typing events, background jobs and errors. Secrets such as PINs and card numbers are masked.

The log is one file per day in your Mac's Logs folder. It is kept for 30 days and capped at about 1 GB. It is never uploaded. The app has no network code. A hidden developer screen behind a passcode check shows the log and can export it as a zip. It goes nowhere unless you share that zip.

What it does not collect

  • No network use in the current app: nothing is sent to any server
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No account or sign-in with any outside service
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or Bluetooth use
  • Full card numbers are not stored (only the last 4 characters entered are kept)

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Logs are kept for 30 days. Database backups keep the newest 14 copies.

Sales and customer data is kept until you delete it, because receipts are meant to be permanent records. An in-app "Erase All Data on This Device" is not built yet. For now you can delete the app's data folder in your Mac's Application Support folder, its Logs folder and its backup folder by hand.

Because the database is a plain file on your Mac, anyone who can sign in to the Mac and open its files can read it.

Children

Mac POS is a business register for shop staff and is not directed at children. It has no accounts, and no data leaves the Mac. Customer records are whatever the shop types in.

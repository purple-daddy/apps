Mac POS support

Mac · In development, not released

What it is

Mac POS is an in-development register app for the Mac. It is meant to run sales, keep customers and items, and print receipts, like the old QuickBooks Point of Sale. It is not ready for real sales.

Getting started

  1. This app is not released, so there is nothing to install for regular use yet.
  2. Do not use it for real sales until the developer says it is ready.
  3. Printing, the cash drawer, stock on hand, setup, the Help chat, Erase All Data and export are not working yet.

Common questions

Is Mac POS part of ShopPOS?

No. It is a separate app with its own database on your Mac. It does not connect to a server.

Does it send my sales or customers anywhere?

No. The current app has no network code. Everything stays on your Mac.

Does it store card numbers?

No. Full card numbers are not stored. Only the last 4 characters entered in the card field are kept with the sale, and a check number is kept whole.

Can I edit or delete a receipt?

No. Receipts are permanent. A mistake is fixed with a linked reversal.

Where are the logs?

In your Mac's Logs folder, one file per day, kept for 30 days.

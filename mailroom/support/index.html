Mailroom support

iPhone, iPad, Mac · TestFlight beta

What it is

Mailroom shows all of your Gmail accounts in one inbox and uses Claude to decide which emails are worth a notification. The rest are sorted into Updates, Social, Promotions and Spam. It also shows your calendars together. Mailroom is read-only: you reply in Gmail.

It needs a Mailroom server, which the person running Mailroom operates. The app connects to that server.

Getting started

  1. Install Mailroom from TestFlight on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Sign in or approve the app on your device with Face ID.
  3. Add a Gmail account. Google's own sign-in page opens and asks for read-only access.
  4. Allow notifications so important emails can reach you.
  5. Optionally allow Calendars to see your Apple and Google calendars together.
  6. Tap thumbs up or down on emails to teach Mailroom what matters to you.

Common questions

Can Mailroom send or delete my email?

No. It only has read-only access to Gmail. You reply and manage mail in Gmail.

Are my emails stored?

Headers and short previews are kept on the live server for 45 days (server backup copies may hold older ones until they rotate out). Email bodies are not saved. They are fetched when you open an email.

What does Claude see?

For each new email, Claude gets the sender, subject, up to the first 1,500 characters of the body, a little thread context, your account note and some of your past thumbs choices. It runs with no tools.

How do I remove an account?

Remove it in Mailroom. That deletes its messages and feedback from the server and revokes Mailroom's access in Google.

How do I erase Mailroom's data on my device?

Open Settings and choose Erase All Data on This Device. It lists what is erased and says nothing on the server, in Gmail or on other devices is touched.

Where are the logs?

In Settings, Logs. You can export them from there.

Mailroom privacy policy

iPhone, iPad, Mac · TestFlight beta · Last updated 2026-10-23

All your Gmail accounts in one inbox, with Claude sorting what matters.

What this app is

Mailroom pulls several Gmail accounts into one inbox and tags each email with the account it came from, such as Personal or Shop. It uses Claude to sort each new email and sends a push notification only for the ones that matter. Mailroom can only read your mail. You reply in Gmail.

Mailroom is not a hosted service. It works with a small server that the person running Mailroom operates on their own computer. Today that is the developer's own server. The developer runs no other backend for this app.

Diagnostic and usage data

Mailroom records diagnostic logs on your device. Each line has the time, device name, app version, a session id, an event type and a short message. Every 10 seconds the app also writes a heartbeat line with the current screen, theme, network status, memory use, battery level and free disk space. Logs stay in the app's own folder, one file per day. They are kept 30 days, with a cap of about 1 GB. They are not uploaded. They leave your device only if you tap Export in the Logs screen. Logs are also copied to Apple's on-device system log.

The developer's server keeps its own daily log file with a heartbeat every 10 seconds and records of its work. They are kept 30 days (compressed after one day) and stay on the developer's own server. The server is built to log ids, counts, categories and timings, not email bodies, subjects or sign-in secrets. Push records include the device name and the last 6 characters of a push token. This is Usage Data in App Store terms. It is used only for fixing problems, and the developer does not send it to any analytics company.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no ad identifier
  • No analytics or crash-reporting libraries
  • No write access to your mail: Mailroom cannot send, delete or change email
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access
  • Email bodies are not saved on the server or in the app
  • No paid Anthropic API key and no account system run by the developer

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Email headers and previews on the live server are deleted after 45 days, and the server clears old ones every day. The server also makes backup copies of its database (one before each update and a nightly copy), and those copies may hold older headers until they rotate out. Daily summaries are kept: the code has no automatic purge for them. Removing an account from Mailroom deletes that account's messages and feedback from the server and revokes Mailroom's access in Google. You can also revoke access any time in your Google account.

Logs are kept 30 days on your device and on the server.

Settings has Erase All Data on This Device. It removes cached mail, settings, the device key, logs and the calendar cache from this device only. It does not touch anything on the server, in Gmail, or on your other devices. To remove Apple and Google calendar copies from the server, use Delete Mailroom's calendar copies in the calendar settings.

Children

Mailroom is not directed at children. It does not ask for or knowingly collect information from children.

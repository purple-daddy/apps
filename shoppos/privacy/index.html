Green Phoenix-ShopPOS privacy policy

iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, web portal · Private beta (TestFlight, internal testers), for one shop · Last updated 2026-10-29

A staff-only point-of-sale and cash-drawer tool built for one independent auto repair shop.

What this app is

Green Phoenix-ShopPOS is an internal tool for one auto repair shop. The shop's staff use it to ring up payments against repair orders, count the cash drawer, track parts and see the day's takings. It is not a consumer app and it is not for the public: you need a staff sign-in from the shop, and it only works with the shop's own server.

The people whose information it holds are the shop's customers (name, vehicle, payments) and the shop's employees (sign-in and activity). Customers do not use the app and have no accounts. All of this data is kept on the shop's own server at the shop, not in a cloud service run by the developer. The app is pre-pointed at that server. It is currently shared only with named testers through TestFlight and has not been released on the App Store.

Diagnostic and usage data

The apps and the web portal record what happens so problems can be found and fixed. Staff are told the apps record their activity.

The iPhone, iPad and Mac app writes a heartbeat line every 10 seconds, even when idle. It says which screen is showing, whether the app is idle or busy, whether the person is signed in, whether it is offline, how many sales are waiting, the network type, and memory, CPU, disk, battery and heat readings. The app also records taps and drags, and pointer movement on the Mac, plus each request to the server (the method, the path without any query, the result and the time). The heartbeat is uploaded to the shop's own server. The detailed tap, drag and pointer log stays on the device unless the person shares the logs or sends a problem report.

The web portal records a heartbeat every 10 seconds, every click (the control's name and the first 40 characters of its label), screen changes, calls to the server with their results and timings, mouse-pointer position about four times a second, scrolling, errors, and how many keys were typed in each field. It is designed not to record which keys were pressed. These records are uploaded to the shop's own server. The sign-in screen and number keypads are left out of the click and pointer trail.

The shop's server also keeps its own heartbeat and a log of every request, including who made it by name and the network address. The Android app shows the web portal inside the app, so it uploads the portal's activity records described above (clicks, pointer positions and key counts) to the shop's server as well.

The Help screen's self-checks run on the device (it uses Apple's on-device language model where available). Settings also has a passcode-protected developer log viewer for the developer's own use; it unlocks no extra features. Nothing is sent from Help unless the person taps Send to support.

PINs, passwords, tokens, keys, card numbers and device push tokens are designed not to be logged. Logs are tied to the signed-in person and the device. They are not sent to any analytics service.

Retention: logs on a device are kept for 30 days, with a size limit of about 1 GB. On the server, heartbeat, pointer and scroll records are kept for 30 days and other activity records for 90 days, then deleted automatically.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No tracking of people across other apps or websites, and no advertising identifier
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software added to the apps
  • No location, contacts, photo library, microphone, health or calendar access
  • No full card numbers are stored; only the last 4 digits, the card brand and an approval reference
  • The apps do not read or store card details themselves; they contain no card reader or payment software
  • No accounts with Google, Apple or any other third party, and no sign-in with them
  • Customers do not sign in and do not use the app

Anthropic (the developer's own tooling)The developer uses an AI coding assistant with read-only access to the shop's reports, error alerts and problem reports to run audits and checks. This is the developer's own tooling and not a feature of the app.

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

On your device: in the app, go to Settings and choose Erase All Data on This Device. It removes the saved server, your sign-in and Face ID PIN, the cached price book, the offline log, waiting reports, app logs and settings, and then the app behaves like a fresh install. It is refused while offline sales are still waiting to upload. It does not touch anything on the shop's server, other devices or iCloud. Deleting the app also removes the data stored on that device.

On the shop's server: financial and audit records are kept as permanent records by design. Sales, payments, drawer and cash movements, store credit, the audit trail and problem reports can only be added to and cannot be edited or deleted; a correction is entered as a new reversing record. Staff accounts are deactivated, never deleted, so names keep showing correctly on past records. Activity logs are deleted automatically after 30 or 90 days as described above. Backup copies older than 60 days are removed, always keeping the newest 14.

The app has no self-service button for a customer or former employee to ask for their data to be deleted. Customers and staff can ask the shop about the information it holds, because the shop runs the server and holds the records. Because financial records are permanent by design, the shop may not be able to remove them.

Children

Green Phoenix-ShopPOS is not directed at children. It is a staff-only work tool that needs a sign-in from the shop. It does not ask for anyone's age or birth date and has no features aimed at children.

Shop POS support

iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, web portal · Private beta (TestFlight), for one shop

What it is

Shop POS is the shop's own point-of-sale tool. Staff use it to ring up payments against repair orders, count the cash drawer, track parts and see the day's takings. It is made for one auto repair shop and works only with that shop's server.

It is a private beta shared through TestFlight with named testers. Everyone who uses it is staff at the shop.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the TestFlight invitation the shop gave you, or open the shop's web portal.
  2. On first launch, follow the welcome and setup. The app looks for the shop's server on the shop's network, and you can change the server later in Settings.
  3. Sign in with the PIN or password the shop gave you. You can turn on Face ID or Touch ID to skip retyping the PIN.
  4. Ring up a payment by finding the repair order, or scan its receipt barcode on iPhone or iPad.
  5. If something goes wrong, open the Help screen in the app and follow the self-checks.

Common questions

I can't sign in or I don't have access.

Shop POS is for the shop's staff only. Ask the shop's manager to give you access or reset your sign-in.

The app says it can't reach the server.

Check that you are connected to the internet or the shop's network. If the problem stays, tap the Can't reach the server button, which appears by itself when a request fails and is always in Settings. It sends a report straight to the developer without going through the shop's server.

What happens if the internet drops while I'm ringing up a sale?

The app keeps working from a saved copy of the price book and holds waiting sales on the device. They upload when the connection returns. Erase All Data is refused while sales are still waiting to upload.

How do I use Face ID or Touch ID to sign in?

It is optional and set per person. After you sign in with your PIN, you can choose to use Face ID or Touch ID next time. Your PIN is kept in your device's secure Keychain, on this device only.

How do I clear everything the app saved on my device?

Go to Settings and choose Erase All Data on This Device. It lists what will be removed and says clearly that nothing on the shop's server or other devices is touched.

A customer asks what information the shop keeps about them.

Customers should ask the shop. The shop holds the records on its own server, and financial records are kept as permanent records by design.

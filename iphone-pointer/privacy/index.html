Pointer by Purple Daddy privacy policy

iPhone (works with a free Mac app) · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet) · Last updated 2026-11-07

Aim your iPhone at your Mac and the cursor follows.

What this app is

Pointer turns your iPhone into a remote pointer for your Mac. You aim the phone at the screen and the cursor goes there; tap to click, press and hold to drag, two-finger tap to right-click. It works with the Pointer Mac app, which is a separate download.

There is no account and no server. The iPhone and the Mac talk directly over Bluetooth.

What it collects or handles

DataWhere it livesWho receives itWhy
Pairing key for each Mac you allowOn your iPhone and on your MacNobodySo only phones you approve can control the Mac.
Settings (look, haptics) and calibrationOn your iPhoneNobodyTo remember your preferences.
Activity log (see below)On your iPhoneNobody. It is not uploaded.Troubleshooting.

Diagnostic and usage data

Pointer keeps a small activity log on your iPhone for troubleshooting. It records what the app was doing and errors, and it is not uploaded anywhere by the app. There is no send-to-support feature in this version.

What it does not collect

  • No account or sign-up
  • No advertising
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools from other companies
  • No internet connection is used

Other companies that receive data

None.

Permissions it asks for

PermissionWhy the app asks
BluetoothTo find and talk to your Mac.
MotionTo know where the phone is aimed.

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Pairings and settings stay on your iPhone until you unpair a Mac or choose Settings > Erase All Data on This iPhone, which removes them and returns the app to its welcome screen. It does not change anything on your Mac; unpair there too.

Children

Pointer is not made for children. It has no accounts and does not knowingly collect any data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. To ask about your data, open an issue (don't include private information) or use TestFlight feedback.

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub (public: please don't post private information) or send feedback from TestFlight if you are in the beta.

Pointer by Purple Daddy support

iPhone (works with a free Mac app) · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet)

What it is

Pointer is a remote pointer: your iPhone controls the cursor on your Mac over Bluetooth. You need the Pointer app on your Mac (macOS 14 or later) and an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Getting started

  1. Install Pointer on your Mac and open it. Allow Bluetooth.
  2. Turn Pointer on under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility so macOS lets it move the cursor.
  3. Open Pointer on your iPhone and follow the welcome screen.
  4. Choose your Mac from the list, allow the iPhone on the Mac, then point at the middle of the screen and tap Center.

Common questions

The cursor does not move.

Open Help in the iPhone app and run the self-check. Make sure the Mac app is open, Bluetooth is on for both, and Pointer is allowed under Accessibility on the Mac.

How do I unpair a Mac?

In Settings on the iPhone, unpair the Mac. Remove the iPhone on the Mac too.

Does it need the internet?

No. The phone and the Mac talk over Bluetooth only.

How do I contact the developer?

Open an issue on the developer's GitHub page for the apps (linked from the apps index), or send TestFlight feedback from the TestFlight app.

Still stuck?

If the app has a Help screen, it runs self-checks first. If that doesn't fix it, open an issue on GitHub (public: leave out private information), or send feedback from TestFlight if you are in the beta.

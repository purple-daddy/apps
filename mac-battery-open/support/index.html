Mac Battery Open support

Mac, iPhone · Not on the App Store yet

What it is

Mac Battery Open shows your Mac's battery percentage, charging state, time left and health in the menu bar. The optional iPhone app shows your Mac's battery when sharing is set up. It needs macOS 14 or later for the Mac app and iOS 17 or later for the iPhone app.

Getting started

  1. Install and open Mac Battery Open on your Mac. The battery appears in the menu bar.
  2. Allow notifications if you want the low-battery and charging alerts.
  3. To see your Mac on your iPhone, turn on sharing and choose your own server or your own iCloud.
  4. Install the iPhone app and enter the same server address.

Common questions

Why does the iPhone app say nothing is set up?

Seeing your Mac on the iPhone needs sharing: a small server you run, or your own iCloud. Without that the iPhone app only explains what to set up.

Is any data sent to the developer?

No. With sharing off nothing leaves your Mac. With it on, data goes only to the address you entered or your own iCloud.

Can I read the code?

Yes. It is MIT licensed and open source.

How do I contact the developer?

Open an issue on the developer's GitHub page for the apps (linked from the apps index).

Mac Battery Open privacy policy

Mac, iPhone · Not on the App Store yet · Last updated 2026-11-07

A small open-source menu-bar app that shows your Mac's battery.

What this app is

Mac Battery Open shows your Mac's battery in the menu bar and alerts you when it drops low, starts charging, is unplugged or is full. An optional iPhone app shows your Mac's battery with a Live Activity. It is open source (MIT licence).

Sharing between devices is off until you turn it on, and then it goes only to a server you run or to your own iCloud, never to the developer.

Diagnostic and usage data

The developer receives no usage data or diagnostics. With sharing off the app makes no network connections at all.

What it does not collect

  • No account or sign-up
  • No advertising
  • No tracking
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools from other companies

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Settings stay on your device. Anything shared to your own server or iCloud is kept there by you; remove it by turning sharing off and deleting it from that server or iCloud. Deleting the app removes its local settings.

Children

Mac Battery Open is not made for children. It has no accounts and does not knowingly collect any data.

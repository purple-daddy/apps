Better Bezos support

iPhone, iPad, Mac (Android beta by direct download) · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store)

What it is

Better Bezos tracks prices of Amazon items you choose and tells you when they drop. It also has Shop, Orders, Browse and Account screens that show Amazon in a cleaner way, a Your data screen for exports and backups, and Siri and Spotlight support. A browser add-on can add a Track price button.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and connect it to your price tracker. The app can find the tracker on your network, or you can use the app without a tracker.
  2. Allow notifications so you get price-drop alerts.
  3. Track an item: use Track Price in the share sheet from the Amazon app, or the Track price button from the browser add-on.
  4. Set a target price and open the item to see its price chart.
  5. If the tracker cannot be reached, tap the report button that appears, or find it in Settings, Troubleshooting. For other problems, open Help and use Send to support.

Common questions

Where does my tracked-item data go?

To the tracker server you connect to. In this beta that is the developer's own server. Nothing goes to a cloud host.

Does the app see my Amazon password?

No. You sign in inside the app's web view like in a browser. The app does not store or read the password. Order contents are read on your device and are not sent to the tracker.

Does it upload logs?

Only if a tracker is set up: then to the tracker server, about once a minute. Without a tracker the log stays on your device. See the Usage Data section of the privacy policy.

Can I use it without a tracker?

Yes. Choose Use without a tracker. Then no data is sent to a tracker.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings, Erase All Data on This Device for the phone (it also clears backup copies and Siri's list, but not a backup folder you chose or anything on your tracker). Data on the tracker can only be removed by the tracker owner.

How do I back up or move my items?

Open Settings, Your data. Save everything to a file, save items or prices as a spreadsheet, or read a file back in. You see what will change before any import, and a safety copy is made first.

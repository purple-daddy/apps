Better Amazon privacy policy

iPhone, iPad, Mac (Android beta by direct download) · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store) · Last updated 2026-10-23

Track the prices of Amazon items you choose and get a notification when one gets cheaper.

What this app is

Better Amazon (shown on the Home Screen as Better Bezos) watches the price of Amazon items you choose and sends a notification when one gets cheaper. It also gives you a cleaner way to search, view orders and browse Amazon. A browser add-on can add a Track price button to Amazon product pages.

Price tracking depends on a small tracker server that its owner runs on their own computer. The apps connect to that server with a key. In this beta, the server is the developer's own.

Diagnostic and usage data

This is Usage Data, and it is uploaded. The app keeps an activity log. It writes a line every 10 seconds, even when idle: app state, current screen, CPU, memory, free disk space, battery and network type. It also records taps and drags (finger paths sampled about four times a second), text-field focus, screen changes, app start and background changes, crash markers and how long requests take. The log also records the address (without anything after a question mark) of each Amazon page you open in the app, and the picture links of the items you track. Each phone is identified by its model plus a random code made at install, not by your name, and the uploaded log is tied to that device code.

The log is saved on your device for 30 days, capped at about 1 GB. It is also uploaded about once a minute to the tracker server, where it is kept 30 days, capped at about 1 GB. The tracker owner, the developer, can read it.

The log leaves out the key, cookies, the contents of web pages, what you type, search words, and order and return contents. Button labels are recorded only on screens that show none of your own data. This is by design, but every log line has not been separately audited.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools from other companies
  • No accounts of its own; the app never reads or stores your Amazon password
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
  • Order contents are never sent to the tracker

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Tracked items stay on the tracker until the owner removes them. Removing an item in the app only marks it as inactive. Price history is kept and is not automatically cleared. Server logs are kept 30 days. Daily database backups are kept 30 days on the tracker owner's computer and copied to another backup of the developer's.

On your device, open Settings and choose "Erase All Data on This Device". It removes your settings, the tracker address and key, your Amazon sign-in and cookies, push registration, saved shares, caches and local logs, and starts a new log identity. It does NOT delete anything on the tracker or your Amazon account. Data on the tracker, including items, price history, your device token and uploaded logs, can only be deleted by the tracker owner, and there is no self-service delete for it yet.

If you use the app without a tracker, none of this server data exists.

Children

Better Amazon is a shopping tool for adults, since Amazon accounts require adults. It has no accounts of its own, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children.

Precinct 2008 support

iPhone, iPad · Private beta (TestFlight)

What it is

Precinct 2008 is a field guide for the inspector of one polling place. It puts the county's 226-page poll worker guide on the phone, split into its nine booklets and searchable, along with checklists that link to the guide's pages, a list of key facts, a schedule and a roster of the inspector's poll workers. A Notable people list holds anyone else you may need to reach, with phone and email, and a Notes area holds anything you want to remember.

It works without a signal. Everything you enter stays on the phone unless you export it or choose a backup folder.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from TestFlight and tap Get started on the welcome screen.
  2. To bring in a roster file, put the file on the phone (for example with AirDrop), then open Settings, then Your data, then Import a Precinct 2008 file, and pick it.
  3. Read the preview that shows what will change. If it looks right, tap Save a safety copy, then replace what's on this iPhone. If not, tap Cancel.
  4. Add people one at a time with the plus button on the People tab. Tap a person to call, text or copy their number, set tags, add notes and tick their checkboxes.
  5. Use Notable people (on the People screen) for anyone who isn't a poll worker, and Notes for anything else you want to remember. Add default checkboxes for every poll worker in Settings.
  6. Use the Checklists tab to tick items off, and tap a page number to open that page of the guide.
  7. In Settings, under Automatic backups, tap Back up now, and optionally choose a folder for extra copies.

Common questions

Does the app need a signal or internet?

No. The guide, its text, the checklists and the roster all work in airplane mode. The app has no code that connects to the internet.

Where is my roster, notable people and notes saved?

Your roster, notable people, notes and checkboxes are on this iPhone, in a file that iOS keeps locked while the phone is locked. You can also turn on an app lock in Settings (Lock with Face ID or passcode; it is off until you turn it on). It goes elsewhere only if you export it, share it or choose a backup folder in Settings.

How do I back up my roster, and how do I restore it?

The app saves a backup on the phone about once a day and before every import or restore, and keeps the newest 30. Settings, then Automatic backups, shows them. Tap Restore next to one to see a preview before anything changes. You can also tap Back up now, or choose a folder such as one in iCloud Drive or Google Drive to get extra copies. Those copies contain names, phone numbers, emails and notes, so choose a folder only you can open.

How do I move my roster to another iPhone or into a spreadsheet?

Open Settings, then Your data, and tap Prepare export files. You get a full export that opens in this app, plus spreadsheet files (people, notable people, checkboxes, schedule, checklists and breaks) and a Notes text file. They contain names, phone numbers, emails and notes, so share them only with people who should have them. The temporary copies are removed when you leave Settings.

What does Ask the guide do?

Type a question on the Guide tab. The app finds the matching pages in the guide. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence it also writes a short answer from those pages, using Apple's on-device model, and names the pages. On other phones it shows the matching pages.

The app says it can't open my saved data. What do I do?

This can happen right after the phone restarts, before it is unlocked. Unlock the phone and tap Try again. If it still fails, you can restore the newest backup. The app keeps the unreadable file as a safety copy first.

How do I erase everything?

Open Settings and choose Erase All Data on This iPhone. It lists what is erased and what is not, then asks you to confirm. It does not touch a backup folder you picked or files you already shared.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Lock with Face ID or passcode. It is off by default. When it is on, the app asks you to unlock it when it opens and each time you come back to it, and its picture in the app switcher is blank. The lock uses the phone's own Face ID or passcode.

Can I keep my roster in my iCloud Drive?

Yes. In Settings, tap Use my iCloud folder and pick a folder (for example one inside iCloud Drive). If a roster file is there, the app shows a preview you can import. From then on the app copies its backups into that folder: after changes (at most every 10 minutes), once a day and before imports. These copies include names, phone numbers, notes and saved screenshots, and are managed by the service that runs the folder, so choose a folder you trust.

How does Add from a screenshot work?

Pick one or more screenshots of an elections-office message with the system photo picker. The phone reads the text on the phone and suggests changes. Nothing changes until you confirm each one. The screenshots stay with the message and are included in backups.

Can I use two windows?

Yes, on an iPhone Duo or an iPad you can open the app in two windows side by side. Each keeps its own tab and Guide page, and the data is the same in both. With the lock on, both windows are covered when you leave the app.

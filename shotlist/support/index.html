Shotlist support

iPhone · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet)

What it is

Shotlist helps you do something with the screenshots you take. It sorts them, summarizes them, finds places on a map and reminds you. It works on your phone, with no account.

Getting started

  1. Open Shotlist and allow access to your Screenshots album when asked.
  2. Wait for the first scan to finish. A Live Activity shows its progress.
  3. Browse your categories, or search for something you saved.
  4. Allow location and notifications if you want place suggestions and reminders.
  5. To save a link, use the Share button in another app and choose Shotlist.

Common questions

Does Shotlist upload my screenshots?

No. Screenshots are analyzed on your phone. The exception is beta builds from TestFlight: if you give thumbs-up or thumbs-down feedback, the app can send the sorter's result and, by default, the screenshot to the developer. The developer plans to turn this off before a public release.

Why does it ask for Always location?

Only to notice when you arrive in a new city and remind you about places you saved there. You can use the app with While Using the App or with no location.

How do I remove one item?

Use Forget on the item. To remove everything, use Erase All Data in Settings.

Where can I see the activity log?

In Settings, under Logs. You can export it from there.

Can I stop it reading new screenshots?

Yes. Use the pause option.

Still stuck?

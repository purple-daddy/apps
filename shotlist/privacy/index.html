Shotlist privacy policy

iPhone · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet) · Last updated 2026-10-23

Turn your screenshots into sorted ideas, places, recipes and reminders.

What this app is

Shotlist reads the screenshots in your Screenshots album and sorts each one into a category such as ideas, recipes or places. It writes a short summary and action steps, puts places on a map, and reminds you about things you saved. Your screenshots are analyzed on your phone.

There is no account and no developer server for your content.

Diagnostic and usage data

Shotlist keeps an activity log on your phone. It writes a short line every 10 seconds, even when idle. It also records events such as scans, notifications, link fetches and place searches. Some of these lines can include a link you shared or the place text that was searched.

The log is kept for 30 days and capped at about 1 GB. It is never uploaded. You can view and export it in Settings, under Logs. It leaves your phone only if you export it yourself, or if you send a support report, which includes a few masked log lines.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No developer server for your screenshots or content
  • No upload of the activity log
  • No iCloud sync, contacts, camera, microphone or health data

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Logs are kept 30 days. The last 14 backups are kept.

To clear the app, open Settings and choose Erase All Data. It deletes your items, scan runs and categories, then clears settings, logs, on-phone backups, tester feedback and waiting queues. It shows an error if anything could not be erased. It does not touch your Photos or other devices. "Forget" removes a single item. Pausing stops the app from reading new screenshots. Deleting the app removes its data from your phone.

A support report or tester feedback you already sent cannot be taken back.

Children

Shotlist is not directed at children. It has no accounts, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. To ask about your data, open an issue (don't include private information) or use TestFlight feedback.

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub (public: please don't post private information) or send feedback from TestFlight if you are in the beta.

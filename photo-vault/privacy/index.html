Photo Vault privacy policy

Mac · Not on the App Store yet · Last updated 2026-10-23

Copy your iCloud Photos library to your own external drive.

What this app is

Photo Vault is a free Mac app that copies your iCloud Photos library to an external drive as an organized archive. Files are named by date and placed in year and month folders. Favorites, albums and places are kept. A viewer web page on the drive opens the archive on any computer without internet.

The app can also help free up iCloud space, but only after it re-checks that the copies are safe and you confirm.

Diagnostic and usage data

Photo Vault records a diagnostic log on your Mac only. It records a heartbeat every 10 seconds, clicks, pointer moves, key events without the typed text, window events, each copy, verify and free-up step, drive connect and disconnect, sleep and wake, and errors. Log lines can include file paths of items on your drive. Tokens are masked. The log is written to a folder under your Mac's user Library and to Apple's system log on your Mac.

The log is not uploaded anywhere. You can export it as a zip from the app. It is kept 30 days and capped at about 1 GB. If you choose to send a support report, a short masked excerpt of the log goes in it, as described above. This is Usage Data in App Store terms, and it is used only for fixing problems.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No tracking
  • No analytics or crash-reporting libraries
  • No account, sign-in or password
  • Your photos, catalog and logs are designed not to be uploaded to a server
  • No contacts, camera, microphone or Bluetooth access, and your current location is designed not to be used
  • Photos in your Hidden album are skipped, not read or copied

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

The archive stays on your drive until you delete it. The app never deletes anything on the drive. A file it replaces is moved to a replaced folder. Logs are kept 30 days.

The only change Photo Vault makes to your Photos library is Free up iCloud. It runs after a fresh re-check and Apple's own confirmation. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted for 30 days.

In Settings, choose Erase All Data on This Device to remove the app's support folder, caches, saved settings and log files. You can choose to keep the catalog safety copies. It does not touch the archive on your drive or your Photos library. To delete the archive, delete the Photo Vault folder on the drive yourself. The app cannot delete a support report you already sent.

Children

Photo Vault is not directed at children. It has no accounts and does not collect personal information from you.

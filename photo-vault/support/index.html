Photo Vault support

Mac · Not on the App Store yet

What it is

Photo Vault makes a complete, organized copy of your iCloud Photos library on a drive you own. You can open the copy later on a Mac or Windows computer with the viewer page on the drive, with no internet.

The archive is not encrypted and has no password. Anyone who has the drive can read it, including photo locations.

Getting started

  1. Connect your external drive.
  2. Open Photo Vault and allow access to your Photos library.
  3. Choose the drive and start the copy.
  4. Wait for the copy and verify steps to finish.
  5. Open Open Photo Vault.html on the drive to browse your archive.
  6. Only if you want to, use Free up iCloud. The app re-checks the copies and asks you to confirm.

Common questions

Does Photo Vault upload my photos anywhere?

No. Photos, catalog and logs stay on your Mac and your drive.

Does it change or delete my photos?

Only if you choose Free up iCloud and confirm. Items go to Recently Deleted for 30 days. The app never deletes anything on your drive.

Why are Hidden photos missing?

Photo Vault skips the Hidden album on purpose.

How do I open the archive on another computer?

Open Open Photo Vault.html from the drive in a web browser. It works on Mac and Windows without internet.

How do I remove the app's data?

In Settings choose Erase All Data on This Device. To remove the archive, delete the Photo Vault folder on your drive.

Where are the logs?

In the app you can export them as a zip.

Mac Battery support

Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet)

What it is

Mac Battery shows how much charge your MacBook has left on your iPhone, iPad, widgets and Apple Watch, and alerts you about low battery or charging. The Mac app lives in the menu bar and also has a notch panel with system information and music controls.

Getting started

  1. Install the Mac app on your MacBook and the iPhone or iPad app on your other devices.
  2. Sign in to the same iCloud account on all of them.
  3. Allow notifications when asked, so you get battery alerts.
  4. Open the app on your iPhone to see your Mac's battery and start the Live Activity.
  5. Add the widget or Watch complication if you want it on your Home Screen or watch face.
  6. If something looks wrong, open the app's Help screen and run the self-checks.

Common questions

Does the developer see my battery data?

No. Battery data moves between your devices through your own private iCloud. The developer cannot read it.

Can I stop the Live Activity?

Yes. The app has a Stop Live Activity button on the screen that starts it and in Settings.

How do I reset the app on one device?

Go to Settings and choose "Erase All Data on This Device". It does not change your other devices or your iCloud data.

What does Send to support send?

It shows you a preview first. If you send it, it includes app and system versions, your device model, network type, Help-chat text, self-check results, an optional note, and a few masked log lines.

Why does the Mac ask about Music or Spotify?

The notch panel shows what is playing and has play, pause and skip buttons. That needs your permission to control those apps.

Still stuck?

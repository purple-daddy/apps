PowerHouse – Battery & Notch privacy policy

Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet) · Last updated 2026-10-29

See your MacBook's battery on your iPhone, iPad, widgets and Apple Watch, plus a handy notch panel on your Mac.

What this app is

PowerHouse shows your MacBook's battery level live on your iPhone, including the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen, and on your iPad, widgets and Apple Watch. It sends you alerts for things like low battery or charging. The Mac menu-bar app also has a notch panel with CPU load, fans and temperature, free disk space, and what is playing in Music or Spotify. PowerHouse was called Mac Battery until October 2026.

The direct-download Mac version (not the TestFlight Mac version) adds more tools to the notch: a Storage tab that shows what is using disk space, file conversion, dictation, and a tab for the developer's Lanes notifications. Everything runs on your own Mac.

The app has no accounts. It uses your own iCloud to move data between your devices.

Diagnostic and usage data

PowerHouse keeps an activity log on each device. It writes a short line every 10 seconds, even when idle. It also records taps and clicks, background jobs, network requests (the address, result and time taken) and errors. Passwords, keys and tokens are masked.

The log stays on the device in files for 30 days, then it is deleted. The folder is capped at about 1 GB. On the Mac the files are in the Logs folder (inside the app's container if the app is sandboxed). iPhone, iPad and Watch also copy their log, in batches of up to 4 MB about every 10 minutes, to your own private iCloud. Your Mac pulls those into its own log folder so you can read everything in one place. The developer's servers do not receive these logs.

You can read the log on the device in a hidden developer screen. A log reaches the developer only if you tap "Send to support", and then only the last few masked lines, after you have seen a preview.

The Storage, Dictation, Convert and Lanes parts of the notch write to the same log, but only counts, sizes, timings and states. The log never contains the words you dictate, the text of a Lanes message, or your file contents. Screen-capture logs record only sizes and counts.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No contacts, photos, camera, health data or location
  • No audio recordings: the microphone is used only while you dictate, and only in the direct-download Mac version

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Logs are kept for 30 days, then deleted.

To clear this device, open Settings and choose "Erase All Data on This Device" (Mac, iPhone, iPad and Watch). It clears settings, setup, waiting alerts and reports, the Live Activity, the Mac's imported push key and the local logs. On the direct-download Mac version it also clears the saved Storage check, your last 50 dictations and PowerHouse's pairing with Lanes. It does NOT touch your other devices, the data in your iCloud, backups you saved, or anything on a server. The device ID in the Keychain is kept. Converted files and anything you moved to the Trash are your own files and are not touched.

Device records in your iCloud are removed automatically after 30 days without activity. You can also remove the app's iCloud data in your device's iCloud storage settings. Backups and exports are files you control. A report you already sent cannot be taken back.

Children

PowerHouse is not directed at children. It has no accounts and the developer does not collect data from anyone, including children.

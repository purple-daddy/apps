Clementine privacy policy

Mac · Not on the App Store yet · Last updated 2026-10-23

A free Mac app that converts pictures, videos, audio and PDFs on your own Mac, designed so that nothing is uploaded.

What this app is

Clementine lives in the menu bar. You drop a file on it, pick a format, and the new file is saved next to the original. The conversion happens on your Mac. Your files are not uploaded anywhere by design.

Diagnostic and usage data

Clementine keeps an activity log on your Mac. It writes a short line every 10 seconds, even when idle, and also records clicks, key presses, pointer movement (about 4 times a second) and each conversion. It is designed not to record what you type. File names and folder paths are removed from the log, and anything that looks like a password or key is masked.

The log is saved in one file per day in your Mac's Logs folder. It is kept for 30 days. Older days are compressed, and the folder is capped at about 1 GB. The log is not uploaded. The app has no automatic upload of logs or usage data.

The log leaves your Mac only if you tap Send in the Help window. Then the last 80 lines, with file names removed, go to the developer inside the encrypted support report. The note and Help chat text you write are sent as you typed them, so don't put personal details in them. The Help chat itself uses Apple's on-device model, so what you type to it stays on your Mac until you choose to send a report.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools from other companies
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No automatic upload of logs, history or settings
  • The contents of your files are not sent anywhere by design
  • No contacts, location, camera, microphone or Photos library access

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Logs are kept for 30 days. Backups keep the newest 14. History keeps the newest 500 entries. Everything else stays until you delete it.

To delete it all, open Settings and choose Erase All Data on This Device. That removes the settings, history, in-app backups, waiting reports, Spotlight and Siri entries and logs. It does not touch your converted files, your original files, or backups you saved to your own folder.

A support report that has already been sent cannot be taken back by the app.

Children

Clementine is not made for children. It has no accounts and no ads. It collects nothing from anyone unless they choose to send a support report.

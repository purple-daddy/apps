Car Mode by Purple Daddy privacy policy

iPhone (made for the iPhone Duo's open screen; also works folded and on other iPhones) · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet) · Last updated 2026-10-29

A car-style home screen for the open iPhone Duo in a car mount.

What this app is

Car Mode turns an iPhone into a driving home screen with big icons, a map with place search and drive times, Now Playing controls for the Music app, phone favorites, messages to your favorites by voice dictation, and optional read-aloud alerts. It is built for the open screen of the iPhone Duo in a car mount, and falls back to a simple list when folded or on a narrow screen.

There are no accounts, no passwords and no ads. It is not Apple CarPlay and does not appear on a car's own display.

Diagnostic and usage data

Car Mode writes an activity log on your device: a heartbeat every 10 seconds, even when idle (screen, layout, connection type, battery level, memory and disk figures, and the number of reports waiting), plus taps and screen changes, background jobs, network requests (what, status and time taken), and errors. Free text is not recorded: contact names, messages, addresses and search text are reduced to a character count or masked, and anything that looks like a phone number is cut to its last 4 digits. Your location is not written to the log.

Logs stay on your device for 30 days (capped at about 1 GB, oldest removed first) and are not uploaded automatically. The only time part of the log leaves your phone is when you send a support report, which attaches a short recent slice of it. The log can only be opened through a hidden developer screen protected by a passcode.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No accounts or passwords
  • Car Mode cannot read your call history, voicemail or messages
  • Your location is not stored in Car Mode's logs or sent to the developer

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Settings, favorites and saved places stay on your device until you delete them. Daily backups are kept on the device (the newest 14 of each kind). Logs are kept 30 days. To remove everything, use Settings > Erase All Data on This Device: it erases your settings, look, shop and alert addresses, favorites, saved places, in-app backups, reports still waiting to send, Spotlight and Siri entries, and the logs, and the app starts fresh. It does not touch your contacts, music, messages or Apple Maps, any server, other devices, or export files you saved to Files or iCloud Drive. Support reports already sent to the developer are not removed by Erase.

Children

Car Mode is not directed at children. It has no accounts, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

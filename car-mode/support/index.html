Car Mode by Purple Daddy support

iPhone (made for the iPhone Duo's open screen; also works folded and on other iPhones) · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet)

What it is

Car Mode is a driving home screen. It gives you big, glanceable icons for Maps, Music, Phone and Messages, a dashboard with a live map, and optional cards for a shop status check and read-aloud alerts. It is not Apple CarPlay and does not show on your car's screen.

Getting started

  1. Open Car Mode and follow the welcome screens.
  2. Allow Location, Music and Contacts if you want the map, Now Playing and favorites.
  3. Optional: in Settings, type a shop address and an alert feed address.
  4. Optional: in Settings > In the car, follow the steps to make a Shortcuts automation that opens Car Mode when your car's Bluetooth connects.
  5. Mount the iPhone Duo open and in landscape for the car layout. Folded or on a narrow screen you get a simple list.

Common questions

Is this Apple CarPlay?

No. Real CarPlay on the phone needs Apple's protected entitlement. Car Mode is an app on the phone's own screen with a similar layout.

Does it open by itself when I get in the car?

Only if you set up a Shortcuts automation. Settings > In the car shows the steps.

Why is my music app not showing?

Car Mode shows and controls the Music app's player. Other apps' audio cannot be shown or controlled.

Can it read my messages?

Not itself. iOS does not allow that. You can ask Siri to read new messages. Car Mode helps you send a message to a favorite using voice dictation.

The shop tile is red.

It turns red when the saved address does not answer correctly within 5 seconds. Check the address in Settings, or tap Can't reach the shop to send a report.

How do I delete my data?

Settings > Erase All Data on This Device.

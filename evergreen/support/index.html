Evergreen support

iPhone, iPad, Mac · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet)

What it is

Evergreen helps you see where your savings stand. You can log money in and out, set money aside for goals, track what you owe, and save together with friends or family in Shared Goals.

Getting started

  1. Open Evergreen and enter your starting balance and currency.
  2. Turn on the Face ID lock in Settings if you want one.
  3. Log deposits and withdrawals as they happen.
  4. Create savings items with a goal, and add money to them.
  5. Add the widget or Live Activity if you want your numbers at a glance.
  6. Use Help to run the self-checks if sync or backups seem off.

Common questions

Does the developer see my money data?

No. It stays on your device and in your own private iCloud. The developer sees something only if you choose to send a support report.

What does Send to support include?

After a preview, it sends the app version, device model, self-check results, an optional note and the last 40 log lines. It is designed to leave out money amounts, names and notes. It is encrypted.

How do I get my data out?

Use Settings, Export My Data. It makes a JSON file with everything and a CSV file of your entries.

How do I restore a backup?

Open Settings, Backups. The app saves a copy daily, and before any erase, restore or import.

What does Erase All Data not remove?

Shared Goals. Delete or leave them under Savings, Shared Goals. Erase does save a backup on your device first.

Still stuck?

Evergreen privacy policy

iPhone, iPad, Mac · TestFlight beta (not on the public App Store yet) · Last updated 2026-10-23

A private savings app: track your balance, set money aside and reach your goals.

What this app is

Evergreen is a personal savings app. You track a balance, log deposits and withdrawals, set money aside in savings items with goals, track debts, and save toward Shared Goals with people you invite. It has a Face ID lock, widgets and a Live Activity.

There is no account. Your data lives on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud.

Diagnostic and usage data

Evergreen keeps an activity log on your device. It writes a short line every 10 seconds, even when idle. It also records taps and screens, app start, stop and background changes, sync events, and crash or freeze information that iOS provides. Money amounts are masked. Names and notes are designed not to be recorded.

The log stays on your device for 30 days. It is not uploaded, and the crash and freeze information is only written to this local log.

A part of that log reaches the developer only if you tap Send in Help, under "Send to support". You see exactly what will be sent first. It is the last 40 log lines plus the app version, device model, self-check results and any note you typed. It is designed to leave out money amounts, names and notes. The report is encrypted and goes through a free public relay.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No tracking
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools from other companies
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data
  • No upload of your money data to the developer

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Logs are kept for 30 days. Backups keep the last 30 daily copies plus the last 10 made before an erase, restore or import. They stay until you delete them in the Files app or delete the app.

To delete your data, open Settings and choose Erase All Data. It deletes your balance, entries, savings items, purchases, debts and the synced wallpaper from this device, your other devices and your iCloud. It first saves a backup on your device, which you can restore from Settings, Backups. Erase does not remove Shared Goals. Delete or leave those under Savings, Shared Goals.

Deleting the app removes its data from the device. You can also manage the app's iCloud storage in your device's iCloud settings. Backup files in the Files app can be opened by anyone who can unlock your device, because the Face ID lock does not cover them. A support report you already sent cannot be taken back.

Children

Evergreen is not directed at children. It has no accounts, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Loopstone privacy policy

iPhone, iPad, Mac · TestFlight beta · Last updated 2026-10-23

A single-player puzzle game where you shoot colored stones to match chains.

What this app is

Loopstone is a puzzle game based on the old game StoneLoops of Jurassica. You shoot colored stones to match chains before they reach the end. The game has new art and sound. The iPhone, iPad and Mac apps show the same game page inside the app.

Diagnostic and usage data

The game keeps a diagnostic log on your device. It writes a line every 10 seconds, even when idle. Each line has the game state, mode, screen, level, score, lives, frame rate and whether the device is online. It also records key presses (the key and the field, not what you typed), pointer position about 4 times a second, launches and errors with a short stack trace.

The log stays on your device. Up to 4,000 lines are held in memory while the game runs, and the last 200 lines are saved. You can view the log under Options, Logs. You can copy it or download it yourself.

The log is designed not to be uploaded, and to our knowledge the game contains no code that sends data to the internet. The developer receives nothing unless you copy the log and send it yourself.

What it does not collect

  • No internet requests from the game or the app
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No purchases or chat
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications

Other companies that receive data

None.

Permissions it asks for

None.

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Your progress and log stay on your device until you delete them. The saved log is limited to the last 200 lines.

To erase it, open Options and choose Erase all data. That removes your progress, settings, backups and log from the game's storage. Deleting the app also removes its saved data. Progress files you exported are copies that you keep, and the game cannot delete them.

Children

Loopstone has no accounts, ads, chat or purchases, and to our knowledge it collects no personal data; it keeps a local diagnostic log. The game is cartoon puzzle shooting with a dinosaur theme.

Loopstone support

iPhone, iPad, Mac · TestFlight beta

What it is

Loopstone is a puzzle game. You aim and shoot colored stones to make matching chains before the chain reaches the end of the track. It has 75 tracks across five worlds: Jungle, Desert, Glacier, Swamp and Volcano.

There are four modes: Classic, Grab and Shoot, and Endless (in either style). There are four difficulty levels and a stone-speed slider in Options and in the pause menu.

Getting started

  1. Open the game and start from the title screen and main menu.
  2. Pick a mode and a difficulty.
  3. Choose a level on the scrolling level map.
  4. Aim and shoot stones to match colors in the chain.
  5. Use Options to change speed and sound, export or import your progress, or see What's new.

Common questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes. It is saved on your device. A daily backup is also kept, and the last 3 are saved.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Open Options and export your progress as text, a file or CSV. Then import it on the other device. Import keeps a copy from before the import.

How do I erase everything?

Open Options and choose Erase all data.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It works offline.

Where is the log?

Open Options, then Logs. You can copy or download it.

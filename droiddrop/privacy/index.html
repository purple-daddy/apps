DroidDrop privacy policy

Mac · Not on the App Store. This feature now lives inside Good Files. · Last updated 2026-10-23

Copy files between an Android phone and a Mac over a USB cable. It is designed so that nothing goes over the internet.

What this app is

DroidDrop copies files between an Android phone and a Mac over a USB cable. It replaces Google's discontinued Android File Transfer. It talks to the phone directly. To our knowledge the app has no internet code. DroidDrop has been merged into the Good Files Mac app, under Window, Android Phone.

Diagnostic and usage data

DroidDrop keeps an activity log on your Mac. It writes a short line every 10 seconds, even when idle. It also records clicks, key presses, pointer movement, USB plug and unplug, each transfer and any errors. File names can appear in the transfer lines. The app does not record the contents of your files.

The log is saved in one file per day in your Mac's Logs folder, and also in Apple's system log. It is kept for 30 days. Older days are compressed, and the folder is capped at about 1 GB.

The log is designed not to be uploaded, and to our knowledge the app has no code that connects to the internet. You can see it in the Logs window and export it as a zip. It goes nowhere unless you share that zip yourself.

What it does not collect

  • No internet connection from the app, by design
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No upload of logs or files
  • No contacts, location, camera, microphone or Photos library access

Other companies that receive data

None.

USB access to the phoneTo read and write the phone's files over the cable.
Fix It buttonIf you click it, the app stops the Mac's camera helper (ptpcamerad), which can grab the phone before DroidDrop does. The app also checks which other apps may be holding the phone.

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Logs are kept for 30 days and then removed. You can delete the DroidDrop folder inside your Mac's Logs folder at any time. Files you copy are ordinary files that you manage yourself.

Deleting a file on the phone always asks first and cannot be undone.

Children

DroidDrop is not made for children. It has no accounts and no ads, and it collects nothing about anyone.

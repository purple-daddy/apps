DroidDrop support

Mac · Not on the App Store. This feature now lives inside Good Files.

What it is

DroidDrop shows the files on your Android phone and lets you copy them to your Mac and back. You plug the phone into the Mac with a USB cable. It works on Apple Silicon Macs.

The same feature now lives in the Good Files Mac app, under Window, Android Phone.

Getting started

  1. Plug your phone into the Mac with a USB cable and unlock it.
  2. If the phone shows a USB notification, tap it and choose File transfer.
  3. If the phone asks to allow access to phone data, tap Allow.
  4. To copy to the Mac, drag files onto your Desktop or a Finder window, or select them and click Copy to Mac.
  5. To copy to the phone, drag files from Finder into the DroidDrop window, or click Copy to Phone.

Common questions

It says Connect your Android phone.

Nothing was found. Use a cable that carries data, since some cheap cables only charge. Try another USB port and avoid USB hubs.

It says my phone is only charging.

On the phone, swipe down, tap the Charging this device via USB notice, and choose File transfer.

It says another app is using my phone.

Click Fix It. If that doesn't help, quit Photos, Image Capture, Samsung Smart Switch, OpenMTP or Android Studio, then click Try Again.

I can't see Android/data or Android/obb.

Android 11 and newer hide these folders from computers. That is normal.

What if a file with the same name is already there?

The app asks you to Keep Both, Replace or Skip. It is designed not to overwrite anything without asking.

Does it send anything over the internet?

No. By design the app has no internet code, and files go over the USB cable only.

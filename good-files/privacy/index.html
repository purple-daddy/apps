Good Files privacy policy

iPhone, iPad, Mac · TestFlight beta (early testing) · Last updated 2026-10-23

Move files directly between iPhone, iPad and Mac over Wi-Fi, a USB cable or a drive. No server in the middle.

What this app is

Good Files sends files straight from one of your devices to another, over the same Wi-Fi, a USB cable or a drive. It also adds a file browser for On My iPhone or On My iPad inside the Files app. The Mac app also has an Android Phone window, which is the former DroidDrop. It lets you browse and copy files from an Android phone over USB.

Diagnostic and usage data

Good Files keeps a diagnostic log on your device. It writes a line every 10 seconds, even when idle, with the app state, current screen, CPU and memory use, free disk space, battery, network type and heat level. It also records taps, clicks and pointer position, how many keys you press in each field (never what you type), app launches, errors and whether the app crashed last time. Names of files, folders and devices are left out. Screens that are not the app's own are written as hidden.

The log stays on your device. On iPhone and iPad it is in the app's own storage. On Mac it is in your Logs folder, one file per day. It also goes to Apple's system log on your device. Each day's file is compressed after a day, deleted after 30 days, and the folder is capped at 1 GB.

The log is not uploaded automatically. The app has no code that does it. It leaves your device only if you send a support report, which contains a short excerpt with the regular 10-second lines and pointer samples removed, or if you export the log yourself.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising
  • No tracking, and no tracking domains
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools from other companies
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No iCloud or CloudKit
  • No location, contacts or Photos library access
  • File contents and file names are never sent to the developer or to any server
  • No automatic upload of logs

How long it keeps data, and how to delete it

Logs are deleted after 30 days. Received files and your history list stay on your device until you delete them.

To erase the app's data, open Settings and choose Erase All Data on This Device. It clears the app's data, your pairings and the saved device ID, and on iPhone and iPad can optionally remove received files. It says plainly that nothing on your other devices is touched. If you only delete the app, the saved device ID can remain in the Keychain until you use Erase All Data.

A support report that has already been sent cannot be taken back by the app.

Children

Good Files is not made for children. It has no accounts and no ads, and it collects nothing about anyone except what is in a support report that a person chooses to send.

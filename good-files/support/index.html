Good Files support

iPhone, iPad, Mac · TestFlight beta (early testing)

What it is

Good Files moves files directly between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. It works over the same Wi-Fi, over a USB cable between an iPhone and a Mac, or through a drive. It also adds a Good Files location to the Files app, so you can receive files without any setup.

Apple does not allow an iPhone and an iPad to transfer files directly over a cable. The app uses Wi-Fi or a shared drive for that. The Mac app can also browse and copy files from an Android phone over USB, under Window, Android Phone.

Getting started

  1. Install Good Files on the devices you want to connect, and open it on each one.
  2. Allow Local Network access when asked, so the devices can find each other.
  3. Pair the devices. Scan the QR code shown on one device, or enter the pairing code.
  4. Pick the files you want to send, or choose the other device and browse its files.
  5. For a cable transfer, plug the iPhone into the Mac and use the USB Cable tab.
  6. For an Android phone, plug it into the Mac and choose Window, Android Phone. Choose File transfer on the phone.

Common questions

Do my files go through a server?

No. Files go directly between your devices, over your local network or the USB cable, and are encrypted while they travel.

Can I transfer between an iPhone and an iPad over a cable?

No. Apple provides no way to do that. Use Wi-Fi, or a shared USB-C drive.

What is the Open mode with no code?

It still encrypts, but it can't confirm who is on the other end. Use a pairing code when you can.

How do I erase the app's data?

Open Settings and choose Erase All Data on This Device. Nothing on your other devices or on any server is touched.

Where are the logs, and are they uploaded?

They stay on your device, as described in the privacy policy. They are not uploaded unless you send a support report.

The Android Phone window can't see my phone.

Use a cable that carries data, unlock the phone and choose File transfer on it. This window is new and has not yet been tested on many phones.
